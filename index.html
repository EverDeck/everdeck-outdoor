About Everdeck

We build outdoor spaces you'll actually live in.

Everdeck Outdoor designs and builds custom decks, swings, pergolas and outdoor structures for homes across Greater Vancouver. Every project starts with your property, your routines and how you actually want to use your backyard — not a one-size-fits-all template.

From framing to final finish, we take a hands-on, detail-first approach to carpentry, so the structures we build hold up to the Pacific Northwest climate and still look right for years to come.

  • Custom-designed around your property and lifestyle
  • Skilled, detail-oriented craftsmanship on every build
  • Functional, attractive outdoor spaces built to be used
